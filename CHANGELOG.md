# What changed

## 0.1.0, 19 October 2026

First public build.

- Free. No account, no server. Your results and your Webshare key stay on your Mac.
- New run: pick a trade and a place, see the squares on the map, set a cap, start.
- Live run: the map lights up square by square, the table fills, one click export to a spreadsheet.
- Your own proxies: a three step guide connects your Webshare account on first open.
- Connector for Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code and Codex, one button each in Settings. Small searches come back to the assistant in one call.
- Spreadsheet cells are safe to open in Excel even when a business is named like a formula.
