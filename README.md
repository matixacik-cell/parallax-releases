# Parallax for Mac

Parallax finds businesses on Google Maps and hands you a spreadsheet: name, phone, website, address, rating. It is free. It runs on your own Mac, through your own Webshare proxies, and sends nothing to us.

## Download

The latest version is on the Releases page: https://github.com/matixacik-cell/parallax-releases/releases/latest

Open the dmg and drag Parallax into Applications.

## First open

macOS will say it cannot verify the app. We do not have an Apple developer certificate yet, so this warning shows for everyone. You get past it once:

1. In Applications, right click Parallax and choose Open.
2. Click Open again in the window that appears.

On the newest macOS the Open button may be missing. Then open System Settings, go to Privacy and Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

After that the app opens like any other and walks you through connecting your Webshare account in three steps.

## Updates

New versions appear on the Releases page. The app does not update itself yet: download the new dmg and drag Parallax into Applications over the old one. Your runs and your key stay where they are.
